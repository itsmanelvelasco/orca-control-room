# The funnel draws its columns and cards

## summary
The Funnel view shows four stage columns with a card per piece of work; what waits on you glows amber. Presses only open the item's chat or focus a worker. The tab stays behind a dev flag until slice 5 adds it.

## entry
- `src/views/funnel/view.tsx`, `panel.tsx`, `demo.ts`: Ideation, Planning, Execution, Shipping with counts; amber cards first, with the question, gate or failed worker; 20 cards per column, then "+N more"; "Unlinked waits" row; "No work yet."; card detail (tasks, workers, gates, PRs, PRD) drawn only when open.
- `funnel.open`: the worker opens a card's wait, else the bot's home chat, else a worker's terminal or workspace. Only `terminal.focus` and `workspace.open`; no new CLI command.
- The Funnel tab shows only with `room.devFunnel` on (only the `funnel` check scenario sets it).
- A stopped worker ends its CLI calls still running: `plugin check` takes 10-15 s, not 65-85 s, and no longer loses its result at the 60 s kill.
- The slice-2 run-list merge test no longer flakes under load (4 s fake calls).

# Funnel graph panel: wires, legend and bot mascots

## summary
A second panel, "Funnel graph", draws each piece of work in its stage column. Dashed wires run from You to each bot, then to its work: amber waits on you, blue is active, grey is done or not started. Each bot shows its Orca mascot or its uploaded picture.

## entry

Wires stop on reduced motion. The panel has a legend, "What the bots are doing" cards and Unlinked waits. Each column shows 20 cards, then "+N more".

# Agents view: every agent, by project, without the CLI

## summary
A new Agents panel lists every agent in every workspace, grouped by project, with its state and how long it has been in it. It works with the Orca CLI missing.

## entry
- `src/views/agents/*`: reads `workspace.list` (skips workspaces with no terminal) and `agents.list` per workspace, 4 at a time; `agent.status.changed` updates rows at once. Remote SSH and paired-server workspaces show agents only, with a host badge.
- 50 rows per group with "Show all"; the worker sends at most 500 per group. `agents.list` answers at most 50 agents per workspace.
- A row press focuses the terminal, or opens the workspace. Titles are cut to one plain line.
- Tests: `test/agents.test.tsx` (17), including the CLI-missing case.
